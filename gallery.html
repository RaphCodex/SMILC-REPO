Media Literacy Event 2023

Ibadan, Nigeria
October 12-14, 2023

This three-day summit brought together educators, policymakers, and technology experts from around the world to address the growing challenges of misinformation and digital manipulation. Our goal was to develop actionable strategies for integrating media literacy into education systems, create networking opportunities for professionals in the field, and showcase innovative tools for fact-checking and digital verification.

The summit featured 35+ speakers, 12 workshops, and 5 panel discussions focusing on critical thinking in the digital age, algorithmic transparency, and ethical digital citizenship.
